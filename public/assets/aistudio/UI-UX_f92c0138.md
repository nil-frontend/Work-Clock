# WorkClock — UI/UX Specification

**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** 12 November 2026

## 1. UX objective

A user should open WorkClock and see the current time immediately. Settings must be discoverable but not compete with the clock. The ad must be visibly separate, stationary in the layout, and never cause a full-screen interruption or cover any time content.

## 2. Navigation model

v1 uses a single primary destination. Do not add a bottom navigation bar or hamburger navigation unless real product growth requires it.

```text
Clock Screen
  ├── Quick Controls (overlay / bottom sheet)
  │     └── Full Settings
  └── Settings
        ├── Display
        ├── Appearance
        ├── Screen & Brightness
        ├── Privacy & Ads
        ├── About WorkClock
        └── Support
```

## 3. Screen inventory

| Screen / surface | v1 | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Clock Screen | Yes | Primary clock display and bottom banner slot |
| Quick Controls | Yes | Fast access to common settings |
| Settings | Yes | Full preferences |
| Privacy & Ads | Yes | Privacy policy, consent/privacy options where required |
| About | Yes | App version, publisher and disclaimer |
| Support | Yes | Support email / help URL |
| Pro purchase | No, later | One-time ad removal and customisation |
| Onboarding | No | Avoid; app should work immediately |
| Login | No | No account system |

## 4. Clock Screen specification

### 4.1 Hierarchy

1. Main time (dominant visual element).
2. Date/weekday (optional but enabled by default).
3. Controls affordance.
4. Anchored adaptive bottom banner region in free version.

### 4.2 Time display

- Read the system time and time zone.
- Use the chosen `System / 12-hour / 24-hour` format.
- Seconds toggle is available in Settings and quick controls.
- Date is locale-formatted.
- Use tabular figures to prevent text-width jitter.
- Do not show “official time”, “railway time”, “GPS time” or similar labels.

### 4.3 Controls visibility

- Show a small, deliberate settings affordance when controls are visible.
- Controls may auto-hide after 4–6 seconds of inactivity.
- A tap on the clock content may reveal controls, but taps on the ad region must not trigger WorkClock controls.
- Avoid long-press-only interaction; users must have an obvious accessible route to Settings.
- Controls do not cover the clock's most important digits.

### 4.4 Screen-awake toggle

- Label: **Keep screen awake**.
- Default: On for the clock experience.
- Helper text: “Prevents automatic screen timeout while WorkClock is open. The power button still turns the screen off.”
- If turned off, Android's usual timeout behaviour resumes.
- Do not imply that the screen remains on after the app is backgrounded or closed.

### 4.5 Ad banner region

- Show only in the Free edition.
- One anchored adaptive banner at the bottom of the Clock Screen.
- Never show at the top, side, as a floating overlay, as an app-open ad or as a full-screen ad.
- Do not show banners in Settings, Privacy, About or Support in v1.
- The banner must not cover system navigation gestures or controls.
- Maintain sufficient minimum clock area. Hide banner in short-height landscape if necessary.
- The banner must not be loaded until UMP/consent state allows ad requests.
- A network failure, no-fill response or user privacy choice must not block the clock.
- The ad SDK may serve animated creatives; content rating and blocking settings should be configured, but do not promise every banner is motionless.

#### Ad slot fallback decision

Preferred initial behaviour: reserve the bottom slot in Free mode to avoid the clock jumping when an ad loads. If no ad is available, keep the reserved region visually quiet and non-interactive; do not show fake ads, house ads, or a repeated error message. Confirm with device tests that the blank area is acceptable. In short-height layouts, remove the ad slot entirely and let the clock use the space.

If usability testing shows a blank region is undesirable, change to a collapsed slot after a short, bounded initial load window, but animate layout changes gently and never trigger frequent relayout.

## 5. Quick Controls surface

Controls exposed:

- Seconds: On/Off.
- Date: On/Off.
- Keep screen awake: On/Off.
- Time format: System / 12-hour / 24-hour.
- Brightness: System / Custom.
- Theme shortcut.
- Full Settings entry.

Do not put an ad inside the quick controls surface. Use normal Android controls and labels.

## 6. Settings screen

### Display

- Time format: System, 12-hour, 24-hour.
- Show seconds.
- Show date and weekday.
- Screen orientation: Follow device / Portrait / Landscape (only implement forced orientation if platform behaviour and user experience are tested; otherwise omit forced orientation in v1).

### Screen & Brightness

- Keep screen awake.
- Brightness: Follow system / Custom slider.
- Full-screen / immersive mode (optional P1).
- OLED care mode (optional P1; include explanatory warning).

### Appearance

- Theme selector.
- Preview of time/date in selected theme.
- Additional fonts/layouts may be Pro later; do not display locked upsell clutter in v1.

### Privacy & Ads

- Privacy Policy link.
- Privacy options link / consent preferences, shown when UMP says it is required.
- Plain-language statement that clock time/settings are local, while the advertising SDK may process device and interaction data as described in the policy.
- No in-app tracking dashboard.

### About

- WorkClock version and build number.
- Publisher name.
- Support contact.
- Disclaimer: “WorkClock displays your device's system time. It is not an official or certified timekeeping instrument.”

## 7. Responsive behaviour

| Window condition | UX behaviour |
|---|---|
| Compact portrait phone | Single-column layout, large but width-constrained time, bottom banner slot |
| Medium/large phone | Larger digits and more vertical whitespace |
| Short-height landscape | Compact date; hide controls when idle; suppress banner if clock would become too small |
| Tablet portrait | Centred clock in a max-width container; banner separated at bottom |
| Tablet landscape | Wide display, centred time, horizontal quick controls when visible |
| Split screen / freeform window | Recompute based on current window bounds; do not force orientation or assume full-screen |
| Font scaling enlarged | Prevent clipping; reduce clock font size if necessary, keep settings readable |

Use Compose Window Size Classes or equivalent adaptive breakpoints, not fixed device model checks.

## 8. Interaction details

| User action | Result |
|---|---|
| Launch app | Clock renders immediately |
| Tap clock area | Reveal/hide controls according to current state |
| Tap Settings | Open settings screen |
| Toggle seconds | Update immediately and persist |
| Change format | Update time display immediately and persist |
| Change theme | Apply immediately and persist |
| Adjust custom brightness | Update Activity window brightness only |
| Select System brightness | Restore `screenBrightness = -1f` |
| Toggle Keep screen awake off | Clear keep-screen-on state while app is visible |
| Press device power button | Android locks/turns off display normally |
| Home / app switch | Clock goes to background; normal screen timeout resumes |
| Return to WorkClock | Read current system time and reapply keep-awake if enabled |
| Disable network | Clock remains fully functional; ad may disappear/not load |
| Tap ad | Handled by the ad network's normal ad click flow; must not be confused with WorkClock UI |

## 9. Empty, loading and error states

The primary clock has no loading state. It always shows the best available local time.

| Situation | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| App first frame | Show time/date immediately |
| UMP consent update pending | Clock remains visible; no ad request until allowed |
| Consent form required | Show the required privacy UI without delaying clock initialization |
| Ad loading | Keep the clock functional and layout stable |
| No ad fill | Quiet empty reserved region or approved collapse fallback |
| Offline | No ad; no clock error |
| DataStore corrupt/unavailable | Use defaults; app does not crash |
| Invalid custom brightness | Clamp to safe range or reset to system |

## 10. Accessibility

- Use semantic text for current time and date.
- Do not force TalkBack to announce every second. Avoid `liveRegion = Polite` on the ticking text unless specific accessibility testing demonstrates it is appropriate.
- Provide labels for all icon-only actions.
- Keep controls keyboard/switch accessible.
- Maintain touch targets of approximately 48dp.
- Avoid low-contrast themes.
- Respect increased font scaling.
- Ensure the ad region is not given a misleading WorkClock control label.
- Verify screen reader navigation does not get trapped by the ad view.

## 12. UX anti-patterns prohibited

- Banner ad layered over the clock.
- Ad that appears suddenly after tapping the clock.
- Ad that moves when user taps controls.
- Auto-opening browser or Play Store.
- Push/notification advertisements.
- Interstitials when opening settings or leaving the app.
- Ad that resembles a settings button.
- Banner refresh implemented as rapid repeated requests.
- Full-screen consent or ad SDK initialization before the clock renders.
- Screen-awake feature hidden behind an ad or paid paywall.

## 13. UX sign-off

Product owner and QA must sign off on: readability from a practical distance, phone/tablet layouts, ad separation, failed-ad appearance, consent flow, brightness restoration, power-button behaviour and accessibility before release.
